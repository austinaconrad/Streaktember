# 🏃 Streaktember One mile a day, every day of September. Live-synced between everybody playing. A single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no npm, no framework. GitHub Pages serves it, Firebase keeps everyone in sync. **What's in the box** - **Log Run** — the one button on the Today screen. Attach a screenshot of your watch or Strava (that's the receipt), optionally a selfie, type the miles and the time as **minutes : seconds**, and post. A live pace readout confirms the numbers as you type. **🕗 Different time** back-dates a run you forgot to post — see below. Two escape hatches live at the bottom of the same sheet: **track live in the browser** (full GPS tracking, but the screen must stay on) and **import a GPX file** (a real route map, at the cost of exporting the file first). - **Feed** — every logged run posts automatically with the route drawn on a real map, plus miles, time and pace per mile. Reactions (🔥 💪 🐐 👏, behind a ☺+ button) and comments. - **Standings** — a ✓ or ✕ for whether each player has got today's mile in yet, plus days completed out of 30, total miles and current streak. - **Mile splits** — optional. **＋ Mile splits**, the chip sitting right under Total miles/Total time, opens a row per mile; the total time fills itself in from the sum, and the splits show as chips on the post with your fastest highlighted. Ignore it entirely and logging is unchanged. - **Badges** — three **Records** with one holder each (fastest mile, best average pace, most miles) and seven **Achievements** anyone can unlock, with progress bars and the faces of everyone who has earned each one. - **Today** — progress ring toward today's mile, your streak, and a September calendar with every day marked done or missed. - **Final** — from October 1 a fifth tab appears with the results: who went all thirty days, final standings with medals, record holders and every badge earned. Built to be screenshotted. - **Profile** — tap your picture in the top right to change your display name or photo, see your own totals, and sign out. - **Player profiles** — tap *anyone's* picture in the feed or the standings for their full page: big photo, rank, their September calendar with each day's mileage, and a stat grid. See [Player profiles](#player-profiles). - **Wrapped** — from Oct 1, a personal end-of-month recap for every player as swipeable story cards. See [Wrapped](#wrapped). - **The group** — a live tile on Today: 🫀 still alive and ✅ in today, each with a fill bar, plus a note saying how many streaks have broken. Taps through to the standings. - **Weather** — tap **🌤 Add weather** in the log sheet and the run gets a pill showing the temperature, conditions and city **at the hour you actually ran** — `⛅ 70° · Columbus, OH` — next to the comment button. Optional, and it remembers your choice so it's one tap the first time and automatic after that. - **Photos** — capped at `--photo-h` (270px) and cover-cropped so one post doesn't fill the screen; **tap any photo to see it uncropped**. A post leads with whatever best represents the run: the drawn map if it was tracked or imported, otherwise the screenshot, otherwise the selfie.
